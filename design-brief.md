# Latchpoint — Design Brief

## Project
Build (1) a lightweight design system and (2) a one-page landing site for Latchpoint LLC, a solo consultancy that helps small/mid-sized businesses put AI and custom software to work — practically, not hype. Site lives at latchpoint.co.

## Brand feel
Pragmatic expert. Confident, calm, credible. The trusted operator who's done it before — NOT a futurist selling buzzwords. Think clean B2B/fintech, not glowing-brain AI.

## Core message
Systems don't fail from lack of energy — they fail from lack of connection. Latchpoint closes the gap between "almost connected" and "actually connected." We don't sell technology; we make it hold.

## Visual system
- **Primary:** Deep Slate Blue #1E3A5F (dominant — trust, stability)
- **Accent:** Signal Amber #F5A623 (sparingly — CTAs, the "latch" highlight only)
- **Support:** Cool Gray #6B7280 · Off-White bg #F8F9FA · Ink #111827
- **Type:** Inter (headings SemiBold, body Regular); JetBrains Mono for any code/product bits
- **Logo:** lowercase "latchpoint" wordmark + mark = open square with one corner latching closed (interlocking L). Works as favicon.
- Rule: never amber on white for body text.

## Imagery
Real screens, clean diagrams, honest photos. NO stock handshakes, NO glowing brains, NO AI clichés.

## Landing page sections (in order)
1. **Hero** — headline (the connection thesis), one-line subhead, single CTA: "Book a free AI audit"
2. **Problem** — the gap: bought the tool, nobody changed how they work
3. **What I do** — short AI audit → find 2–3 highest-value opportunities → implement at fixed price
4. **Outcome** — "clients typically save 10+ hours/week within the first month"
5. **About** — founder-led, the latchpoint story (brief)
6. **CTA footer** — book a call (calendar link)

## Constraints
- One CTA repeated, not many competing buttons
- Mobile-first, fast, minimal
- Copy: plain over clever, specific over vague, no exclamation points

## Deliverables
Design tokens (color/type/spacing) + a single responsive landing page ready to publish.
